Press release

Replay Amigo: the free iPhone app that rewinds the last thing you didn’t catch, in 12 languages

Finland, November 10, 2026. Anyone learning a language knows the moment: the show moves on, the joke lands for everyone else, and the line you missed is gone. Rewinding the TV isn’t always possible, and on the radio or in a café it never is. Replay Amigo, a new free app for iPhone, keeps the last 60 seconds of what you hear in memory. One tap on “What was that?” shows the last phrase, transcribed in the original language, with the translation waiting until you want it.

The app is built for learners. The original comes first so you try to understand before you look. You can replay the phrase at 0.75× speed, step back through earlier phrases, and tap underlined words for short definitions. With Apple Intelligence, “Explain” tells you what a whole sentence means and why it’s said that way. Phrases you save become flashcards with the real voice from the show; you answer out loud and the app checks the meaning, then brings each card back just before you would forget it.

Everything happens on the iPhone, using Apple’s on-device speech recognition, Translation framework and Foundation Models. There is no account and no server: the audio is continuously overwritten in memory and never leaves the phone. Once the languages are downloaded, it works offline, on a plane or abroad.

“I live with Spanish around me, and I kept missing the one line that mattered,” says developer Ilpo Hokkanen. “I wanted a rewind button for real life that respects privacy and doesn’t cost anything. Replay Amigo will stay free.”

Replay Amigo is available for free on the App Store for iPhone with iOS 26. It understands Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Dutch, Swedish and Finnish.
